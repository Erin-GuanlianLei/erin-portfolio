Erin Lei — Case Study

A Spring Food Bar

Digital & Social Media Marketing Strategy

↓ Download Full Analysis Report (PDF)

Target Audience

The target audience includes two key segments. The first group is local families living in Hobart aged 25–55 who prefer clean, safe, and spacious restaurants and are open to trying different cuisines. The second group is Chinese consumers in Hobart aged 18–40, including international students and migrants, who look for authentic Chinese food for social gatherings. Both groups value food quality, a comfortable dining environment, and shared dining experiences.

A key problem is finding restaurants in Hobart that feel both authentic and clean and are suitable for group dining. These audiences commonly use TikTok and Instagram for food discovery and prefer short, casual, and visually engaging video content.

Objective

Increase dine-in customer traffic at A Spring Food Bar by improving local awareness through short-form video content on TikTok and Instagram Reels within four weeks. This focuses on the awareness and action stages of the consumer journey, where audiences discover the restaurant through social media and are encouraged to visit in person.

Core Message

A Spring Food Bar offers clean, high-quality, and authentic Chinese food in Hobart, suitable for family meals and group dining.

Platform Selection

TikTok and Instagram Reels are selected because they are highly popular among the target audiences and effective for food discovery. These platforms prioritise short-form engaging content and algorithm-driven distribution, which helps reach users based on interests and behaviour. The short video format also suits a creative opening and makes it easy to present the restaurant’s location, environment, and food quickly and clearly.

Content Structure (AIDA Model)

  • Attention: Created through a POV-style opening with a meme-based visual hook and a creative transition that shifts from the imagined scenario to the real restaurant.
  • Interest: Developed by presenting the restaurant location and showing the dining environment in a clear and observational style.
  • Desire: Built through menu and food visuals supported by personal comments from a Chinese consumer perspective on authenticity and taste. Bilingual captions are used throughout the video to make the content accessible to both English-speaking families and Chinese consumers.
  • Action: Encouraged through a direct recommendation and an invitation for viewers to visit the restaurant for group dining.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Video views measure overall awareness. Watch time indicates engagement and content retention. Saves and comments reflect interest in the restaurant. Profile visits and location searches suggest stronger visit intention. These metrics represent different stages of audience response and help evaluate whether the video successfully drives awareness and increases dine-in traffic.
